# 🚀 Easy Social Media Setup Guide

## Connect Your Social Media Accounts in 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Access the AI Marketing Agent

1. Go to `http://localhost:3000/ai-marketing-agent`
2. **Sign in with your Google account** to access all features
3. Scroll down to the "Connect Social Media Accounts" section

### Step 2: Connect Your Accounts

#### 🔵 Twitter

1. Click "Connect" next to Twitter
2. A popup window will open with Twitter's authorization
3. Click "Authorize" to connect your Twitter account
4. Your Twitter account is now connected! ✅

#### 🔵 LinkedIn

1. Click "Connect" next to LinkedIn
2. A popup window will open with LinkedIn's authorization
3. Click "Authorize" to connect your LinkedIn account
4. Your LinkedIn account is now connected! ✅

#### 🔵 Telegram (Easiest Setup)

1. Click "Connect" next to Telegram
2. Follow the step-by-step instructions in the modal:
   - Open Telegram and search for `@BotFather`
   - Send `/newbot` command
   - Choose a name for your bot (e.g., "My Marketing Bot")
   - Choose a username ending with "bot" (e.g., "mymarketingbot")
   - Copy the bot token provided by BotFather
   - Add the bot to your channel as an administrator
   - Enter the bot token and channel ID in the form
3. Click "Connect Bot"
4. Your Telegram bot is now connected! ✅

### Step 3: Start Using AI Marketing

Once your accounts are connected:

1. **Enter a website URL** in the "AI Content Analysis & Generation" section
2. **Click "Analyze & Generate Content"** - AI will analyze the website and create optimized content
3. **Select platforms** where you want to post (only connected accounts will be available)
4. **Click "Post with AI Optimization"** - Your content will be posted automatically!

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Twitter Connection Issues

- Make sure you're logged into Twitter in your browser
- Check that you have a valid Twitter account
- Try refreshing the page and connecting again

### LinkedIn Connection Issues

- Ensure you're logged into LinkedIn
- Make sure your LinkedIn account is active
- Check that you have a public profile

### Telegram Bot Issues

- Verify the bot token is correct (copy from @BotFather exactly)
- Make sure the bot is an administrator of your channel
- Check that the channel ID is correct (format: @channelname)

### General Issues

- Clear your browser cache and try again
- Make sure you're signed in to the AI Marketing Agent
- Check that all required fields are filled
